## Product and category imagery
For production, prefer real product/category photography from the Supply 4 Me catalogue/CMS/backend.

If real category images are not yet available:
1. use neutral temporary photography without false brand endorsements;
2. keep the image component swappable;
3. do not invent product availability.

## App visual
Use an actual current Supply 4 Me customer-app/PWA screenshot inside a device frame when available.
Do not use an Apple App Store badge or Google Play badge.
Do not suggest the app is listed in either store unless that is actually true.
