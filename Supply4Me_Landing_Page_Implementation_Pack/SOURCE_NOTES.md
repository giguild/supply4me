# Supply 4 Me — source-grounded product notes

Use these notes to keep public-facing copy aligned with the existing business concept.

## What Supply 4 Me is
Supply 4 Me is an FMCG distribution business and trusted supply-chain partner connecting businesses with quality products, reliable distribution and growth opportunities.

The company is intended to serve business buyers including:
- supermarkets
- provision stores
- restaurants
- hotels
- pharmacies
- wholesalers
- informal traders

The first operating focus is Benin City, with planned expansion across Edo State, neighbouring regions and ultimately Nigeria.

## Product / platform model
The production platform is a responsive web application / PWA supporting:
- customer registration
- B2B catalogue browsing
- pricing and ordering
- invoices / pro-forma documents
- bank-transfer payment evidence
- payment verification
- inventory and warehouse fulfilment
- deliveries and drivers
- proof of delivery
- customer credit
- reporting and audit
- multi-location operations

The customer journey can begin from Supply 4 Me's official WhatsApp channel, referral, advertising, QR code, social media or direct link.

## Company direction
Vision:
To become Nigeria's leading technology-driven FMCG distribution company.

Mission:
To build the easiest and most reliable wholesale distribution network across Nigeria.

Tagline:
MOVING BUSINESS. GROWING TOGETHER.

## Landing-page implication
The public landing page should sell trust, ease of ordering, genuine FMCG access, dependable fulfilment and business growth—not present Supply 4 Me as a generic consumer shopping site.

The strongest audience emphasis is B2B retailers and business buyers while keeping the experience simple enough for smaller merchants.

## Important claims discipline
Do not publish unsupported statistics such as retailer counts, product counts, number of states served, or delivery percentages unless the business has verified those figures.
